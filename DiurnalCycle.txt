Use:

Q(t,E) = E × [B + A₁e^(-(t-μ₁)²/(2σ₁²)) + A₂e^(-(t-μ₂)²/(2σ₂²))]

Where:

t = time (0–24 h)
E = Environment Condition Factor
B = 0.3
A₁ = 1.3, μ₁ = 7.0, σ₁ = 1.5
A₂ = 1.2, μ₂ = 19.0, σ₂ = 2.5

Environment Condition Factor:

E = F_temperature × F_humidity × F_season × F_weather

Typical E:
0.85 = unusually low demand
1.00 = normal
1.10 = hot/high demand
1.20 = very hot
1.30 = extreme conditions

Finally normalize the base curve so its 24-hour average = 1.0, then multiply by E
This keeps the normal human water-use pattern while letting environmental conditions raise or lower the entire demand curve.
